2025 Kaleidoscope Kaleidoskop 2025

Good morning to all!👋 Thank you for the opportunity and trust given to me to once again handle the GMAHK Paal Dua Manado 2025 kaleidoscope video project. 🙏 📷 If any of you have video documentation or photos related to all activities/memories from the GMAHK Paal Dua Manado since the church was founded until now, please upload them to the Google Drive link provided at the bottom of this page. 📁 Inside that Google Drive, you'll find several year folders. If the year you're looking for isn't there, please add the year yourself via the link or contact me so I can add it for you. 📂 Once inside the year folder, please choose the next folder that matches the activity/memory you're looking for (example: Retreat Jemaat 2024 - 1 January 2024). If the folder you need isn't available, please add a new one with a title that is Short, Concise, and Clear, following the format in the example above. It's better if you can include the full date, but if only the month is available, that's okay too. This will help ensure all documentation is properly organized. ⚠️ Attention! Please be very careful when creating, deleting, or moving folders and files so that documentation already uploaded by others is not disturbed, disorganized, or deleted 🙏 ⏰ The deadline for submitting video and photo documentation is Wednesday, October 15, 2025, as we need time to process everything before the start of the Sabbath 🙏 🆘 If you need help or have any trouble uploading your video/photo documentation to the Google Drive link, you can contact me directly on Whatsapp using the following format: "Full Name - Documentation Year - Title of the Activity/Memory in the Documentation" That's all for the announcement. Thank more you for your cooperation, and God bless! 🙏 Roger Wagiu
